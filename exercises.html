Exercise Library

Reference lifts used across the split. Filter by category.

💪

Bench Press

Horizontal press targeting chest, front delts, and triceps. Primary heavy push movement.

💪

Overhead Press

Vertical press for shoulders and triceps. Performed standing with a barbell.

🦵

Deadlift

Posterior chain hinge. Hits back, glutes, and hamstrings as the main pull day anchor.

🥉

Weighted Pull-Ups

Vertical pull for lats and biceps. Add weight once bodyweight sets exceed 10 reps.

🥉

Barbell Rows

Horizontal pull for mid-back thickness. Keep torso around 45 degrees.

✌

Lateral Raises

Isolation for side delts. Light weight, high reps, strict form.

🦴

Back Squats

Primary lower body strength movement. Quads, glutes, and core stability.

🦴

Front Squats

Quad-dominant squat variation with upright torso demand.

🦵

Romanian Deadlifts

Hamstring and glute hinge with controlled eccentric emphasis.

⚔

Hip Thrusts

Glute-focused hip extension. Drive through heels, squeeze at the top.

👠

Walking Lunges

Unilateral leg work for quads, glutes, and balance.

👠

Calf Raises

Standing and seated variations. Pause at the top, full stretch at the bottom.
